# iKhataPro Commercial Demo Guide (5-7 Minutes)

## Storyboard & Demo Strategy
This guide provides a structured 5-7 minute demonstration script showcasing **iKhataPro** to merchants, hackathon judges, or commercial stakeholders.

---

## 🎬 Demo Step-by-Step Script

### Step 1: Workspace Authentication & Gateway (30s)
- **Action**: Navigate to `http://localhost:3000/#login`.
- **Narration**: *"iKhataPro provides a dedicated multi-tenant workspace for each shop. Let's sign in to our demo shop, LJS Jewellers."*
- **Click**: Click `💎 LJS Jewellers & General Store` chip for 1-click login.

### Step 2: Executive Dashboard & Health Score (45s)
- **Action**: View top KPI cards (Today's Sales, Received Today, Customer Receivables, Supplier Payables).
- **Narration**: *"The dashboard provides live financial metrics computed directly from store transactions. Here we see our Business Health Score of 82/100 and real-time Smart Alerts."*

### Step 3: Customer 360° CRM & Receivables Aging (60s)
- **Action**: Click **Khata** in sidebar -> Select customer *Rahul Sharma*.
- **Narration**: *"Our Customer 360 CRM shows Rahul Sharma's Trust Score of 85/100, lifetime purchases, receivables aging bucket, and payment timeline."*

### Step 4: POS Sale & Automatic Stock Deduction (60s)
- **Action**: Click **POS Counter** in sidebar. Add 2x *Silver Payal 50g*, select customer *Rahul Sharma*, choose *Credit (Gave)*, click **Complete Sale**.
- **Narration**: *"When a POS sale is completed on credit, three things happen instantly: catalog inventory auto-deducts, Rahul's Khata updates, and the sale is recorded."*

### Step 5: GST Invoice & PDF Generation (45s)
- **Action**: Click **Invoices** in sidebar -> Select `INV-2026-001`. Click **View PDF Invoice**.
- **Narration**: *"iKhataPro automatically computes Intra-State CGST+SGST or Inter-State IGST, rendering professional tax invoices ready for PDF download or WhatsApp sharing."*

### Step 6: Accrual P&L vs. Cash Flow Analysis (45s)
- **Action**: Click **Profit & Loss** in sidebar. Toggle between **P&L Statement** and **Cash Flow**.
- **Narration**: *"We strictly decouple Accrual Profit (Revenue - COGS) from Cash Flow (Money In vs. Money Out). Credit sales do NOT falsely inflate cash until collected."*

### Step 7: Data-Grounded AI Copilot (45s)
- **Action**: Click **AI Assistant** (`🤖`). Ask: *"Aaj ki sale kitni hai?"* or *"Rahul se 5000 rupees aaye"*.
- **Narration**: *"Our AI Copilot answers natural language queries strictly using real store data with zero hallucination, prompting explicit confirmation before financial actions."*

### Step 8: Data Safety & 1-Click Backup (30s)
- **Action**: Click Settings (`⚙️`) -> **Data Safety & Backup** -> Click **📥 Download Backup JSON**.
- **Narration**: *"Merchants maintain full control over their data with 1-click structured JSON backups."*
