# iKhataPro Presentation Deck Content (12 Slides)

## Slide 1: Title Slide
- **Title**: iKhataPro
- **Subtitle**: AI-Powered Local Business Management Platform
- **Tagline**: Khata. POS. Inventory. GST. Financial Intelligence. One Platform.

---

## Slide 2: Problem Statement
- **Fragmented Bookkeeping**: Small merchants manage Khata, POS, stock, and GST in separate notebooks or disconnected apps.
- **Consequences**: Delayed collections, inventory stockouts, inaccurate profit calculations, and poor business visibility.

---

## Slide 3: Proposed Solution
- **Unified Platform**: iKhataPro integrates Customer Ledgers, POS Billing, Stock Inventory, GST Invoices, Accrual P&L, Cash Flow, and AI Intelligence into a single local-first operating system.

---

## Slide 4: Unified Workflow Architecture
- Customer -> Khata -> POS -> Inventory -> GST Invoice -> Financial Reports -> AI Copilot.

---

## Slide 5: Digital Khata & Customer 360° CRM
- Real-time customer credit (GAVE) & payment (GOT) ledger.
- Dynamic Trust Scoring (0-100), Receivables Aging buckets, and Promise-to-Pay tracking.

---

## Slide 6: High-Speed POS Counter & Inventory
- Instant product search, stock auto-deduction, dynamic UPI QR codes.
- Inventory intelligence with low-stock alerts, auto-restock modal, and profit margin analysis.

---

## Slide 7: Financial Accuracy & GST Compliance
- B2B & B2C tax invoices supporting Intra-State (CGST + SGST) and Inter-State (IGST).
- Accrual P&L (`Revenue - COGS = Gross Profit`) strictly decoupled from Cash Flow collections.

---

## Slide 8: Data-Grounded AI Business Copilot
- Natural language query engine answering sales, profit, stock, and collection queries.
- **AI Action Safety Guard**: Explicit confirmation required before financial mutations.

---

## Slide 9: Enterprise Security & Data Safety
- Scoped Multi-Tenant Isolation & Role-Based Access Control (Owner, Manager, Accountant, Cashier).
- Soft deletion engine (`isDeleted`) preserving historical audit trails.
- 1-Click structured JSON backup export & schema-validated restore.

---

## Slide 10: Local-First Technology Stack
- Single Page Application built with HTML5, CSS Tokens, ES6 JavaScript, and LocalStorage.
- PWA Service Worker v2 providing offline shell resilience with 0 recurring cloud costs.

---

## Slide 11: Business Impact
- Single unified workspace eliminating manual duplicate entries.
- Real-time receivables aging reducing bad debt risk.
- Instant GST compliance and 1-click PDF sharing.

---

## Slide 12: Conclusion & Q&A
- **Summary**: iKhataPro is a complete, certified, local-first business management platform ready for commercial operation.
